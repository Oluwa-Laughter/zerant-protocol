# Zerant Product

Use when deciding which Zerant features to build and why they belong in the product.

Read PRODUCT.md and the relevant current architecture or scope documents first.

Rules:
- Support organizations, communities, verifiers, credential holders, and integrators with reusable trust and consent primitives.
- Keep credential and payment authority separate.
- Holders should be able to review exactly what is disclosed; verifiers should receive only the requested result.
- Favor a complete, secure, repeatable trust flow over feature count.
- Reject features that weaken privacy or blur account, reputation, and spending boundaries.
- Never turn reputation into one universal social score.
- Distinguish production behavior, experimental integrations, and future capabilities.
