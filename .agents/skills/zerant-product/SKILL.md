# Zerant Product
Use when deciding what Zerant should build, who it serves, or what belongs in a milestone.

Read PRODUCT.md and current scope first.

Rules:
- Initial wedge is developer ecosystems, OSS communities, grants, hackathons, accelerators.
- Holders are free; B2B verifier/issuer infrastructure is the long-term revenue path.
- Prefer one complete privacy-preserving trust flow over broad feature count.
- Reject features that weaken the privacy thesis or are unrelated to the active milestone.
- Never turn reputation into one universal social score.
- Distinguish demo value from protocol value and business value.
